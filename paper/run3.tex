\section{Abstention dominates both the benchmark and the representation}
\label{sec:run3}

Section~\ref{sec:intro} argued that bias benchmarks cannot separate a model that
answers fairly from one that evades. On BBQ~\citep{parrish2022bbq} this holds
twice: evasion is the majority behaviour, and the dominant component of any
direction extracted from a BBQ bias contrast.

\paragraph{Method.}
For each of ten BBQ categories we generate one completion per ambiguous item
($n{=}400$; $232$ for Sexual\_orientation) from \texttt{Yi-6B-Chat} and
\texttt{Qwen1.5-14B-Chat}, $9{,}664$ generations in total, with answer options
withheld from the prompt. A judge (\texttt{gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18}) sorts each
completion into stance, refusal, distractor or unparsable; it passes an
order-swap qualification at $0.990$/$1.000$ against a $0.95$ bar. We form $V_C = \mathbb{E}[h \mid \text{stance}] - \mathbb{E}[h \mid
\text{refusal}]$ from the residual stream at all layers at the final prompt
token, and measure reproducibility as the norm-weighted split-half cosine over
$400$ stratified splits. A direction reproduces iff its floor's lower bound
exceeds a label-shuffled control's upper bound. Categories with fewer than $32$
items in either arm are \textsc{untestable}: no contrast exists to split, which
is not evidence of no direction.

\paragraph{Evasion is the majority behaviour.}
Qwen1.5-14B declines on $84.3$--$100\%$ of ambiguous items, producing
\emph{zero} stance responses in 400 generations on Race\_x\_gender; Yi-6B
declines less but leaves $54$--$68\%$ of completions unparsable, and its
judge-versus-heuristic agreement of $0.618$--$0.802$ indicates systematically
ambiguous output a four-way scheme discards, not noise. Only 5 and 2
of 10 categories are testable, and no race-related category is testable in either
model. Where a contrast exists, extraction succeeds: all seven cells reproduce,
the tightest at $0.721$ against a shuffled bound of $0.096$.

\paragraph{The shared structure is largely refusal.}
Directions across categories are similar (median off-diagonal $0.650$ and
$0.620$; permutation nulls $-0.005$ and $0.009$, $p=0.001$), which invites a
common-mechanism reading. But $V_C$ subtracts a refusal arm, and cosine measures
angle from the origin, so a shared additive term drives pairwise angles together
while leaving differences untouched. Orthogonalising a refusal reference taken
from the \emph{disambiguated} arm --- where declining is over-abstention, not
epistemic caution, and which avoids the circularity of pooling the ambiguous arm
--- removes $50.7\%$ of Yi-6B's shared structure, against $4.8\%$ for a
norm- and covariance-matched random direction. The removal is targeted.
The two models straddle a pre-declared $0.500$ retention bar by under $0.09$; we
report both, noting that Qwen1.5-14B's two testable categories yield a single
pair, and that orthogonalisation is a lower bound since the reference is itself
measured only to $0.821$.

\paragraph{Uniform steering has no operating window.}
At every dose preserving task accuracy ($\alpha \leq 0.10$), no injected
direction shifted the stereotype margin distinguishably from zero. The two
shifts that clear zero occur where disambiguated accuracy falls from $0.617$ to
$0.317$ and a matched random direction produces larger shifts still; at
$\alpha \in \{0.5, 1.0\}$, $100\%$ of $3{,}200$ generations were unparsable
across all four arms including both controls. Applying a direction at uniform
strength leaves no dose at which the model is both intact and moved --- the
failure Section~\ref{sec:adaptive} addresses. This null is the control against
which adaptive gains should be read.
