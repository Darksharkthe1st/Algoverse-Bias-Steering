The paper

CoCoNot built contrastive counterparts for its refusal categories but explicitly not for indeterminate/subjective ones — we build that missing contrast set (benign comparative questions that do have a right answer) and test whether hedging on them is controlled by the same mechanism as harm refusal.

Interpretability as a Science @ NeurIPS 2026 (Sydney), long track ≤9pp, non-archival

The experiment

Partial directional ablation x − λ(x·r̂)r̂ at λ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1}, for r̂_stance, r̂_harm and a covariance-matched r̂_random, on both batteries. θ is the angle between the two trajectories in logit space — under a shared knob the ratio of logit changes is constant across λ and across directions, and that invariant is what probability space destroys.

What a small θ can establish

“Consistent with a shared-control model, within the resolution of this experiment.” Conditional on all four gates passing.

What it cannot

It does not establish a unique shared mechanism. And a large θ licenses nothing on its own — every identified nuisance biases θ toward “distinct”, none toward “shared”. Inference is deliberately asymmetric.

Current gate

G1 is the only current gate, it is NOT satisfied, and it has never been run on a submission model. It is now evaluable on one, which it was not before.

G1 requires all three legs, on Qwen/Qwen3-8B @ b968826d9c46, computed from the model's own activations (contract §12 A6, PREREG §7a): G1a the direction is estimable — split-half cosine beats its label-permutation null and clears 0.68; G1b full ablation cuts refusal by ≥ 0.15 on held-out harmful_test; G1c permuted and covariance-matched random directions each move it by < 0.05 and sit ≥ 4 SE below r̂_harm. All three, or no claim.

The founding hypothesis

The asymmetry that founded the revival

D-008 · 2026-08-17

Geometry is not sufficient evidence

Do not use cosine or principal angle between DiM directions as evidence of functional separation.

Wollschläger et al. (arXiv:2502.17420) define representational independence precisely because orthogonality does not imply independence under intervention. Joad et al. (arXiv:2602.02132) find eleven refusal categories geometrically distinct (cos 0.4–0.6) yet behaviourally collapsed onto "a shared one-dimensional control knob."

D-011 · 2026-08-17

Logit space is the identification

Primary statistic is the angle between ablation trajectories in logit space, not probability space; λ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1}.
