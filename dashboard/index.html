Soft Refusal — the geometry of taking a side

Testing whether the 'soft refusal' direction is real — by showing an input perturbation nobody designed moves models along it, and that removing that component abolishes the behavior change.

⏳ 27 days to submission — Aug 29 AoE In-distribution steering verified Honest negative: no OOD transfer 'Soft refusal' still unclaimed Darksharkthe1st/Algoverse-Bias-Steering
Project overview

What we're building

An activation-space account of soft refusal — the behavior where a model declines to take sides on controversial-but-not-harmful prompts. We extract steering vectors/subspaces with TransformerLens (difference-in-means and 2026-grade upgrades), steer open-weight models toward opinionation or neutrality, and test how soft refusal relates to the Arditi hard-refusal direction.

Why it was revived

The 2025 Algoverse run got a real result — bidirectional in-distribution steering with coherence preserved — but stalled on OOD transfer and ran out of team. Revived Aug 2026 by a three-person team. The frontier moved (refusal is now cones/subspaces, not single directions; labs ship persona vectors and even-handedness evals), yet the exact soft-refusal claim is still unclaimed.

The research question

Is the soft-refusal direction an artifact of the intervention that found it? Every steering paper faces this (non-identifiability, arXiv:2602.06801): a vector that responds to your own push is not evidence you found the model's representation. Our answer is convergent validity — a single-character substitution forcing byte-fallback tokenization, designed with no reference to any direction, displaces activations preferentially along d_soft; the signed projection predicts which prompts flip; and nulling that component abolishes the flip while injecting it reproduces it. Full plan: docs/2026-08-02_sprint_decision.md.

The point: a vector that moves when you push it proves little (non-identifiability, arXiv:2602.06801). A vector that also moves when a perturbation nobody designed flips the model — and whose removal abolishes that flip — is a property of the model. Controls that are never cut: byte-identical retokenization, covariance-matched random-direction null, ASCII-typo, an OOD-direction competitor, and a length/verbosity direction. Nearest prior work (arXiv:2606.07696) asks whether steering survives perturbation; we ask where the perturbation goes.
